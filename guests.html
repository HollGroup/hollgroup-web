Everything You Need to Know

Your stay, made simple.

Easy Check-In

Self check-in via smart lock or key. Full instructions and key collection details are sent in advance through your personal Guest Portal. Check-in from 15:00.

Your Guest Portal

Every booking includes access to your personalised Guest Portal — apartment guide, appliance instructions, local recommendations, and direct contact with our team, available 24/7.

Local Team, Always Available

We are based locally and available throughout your stay. Need airport transfer recommendations, restaurant tips, or help with something in the property? Just ask — any time.

Check-Out & Extensions

Standard check-out is 10:00. Late check-out may be available on request — fees apply. Weekly and monthly discounts are available for longer stays.

Work Stays & Invoices

Staying for work? We can provide a formal invoice for expense purposes. Just let us know at the time of booking and we will arrange it.
