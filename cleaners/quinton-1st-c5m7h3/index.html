NEVER leave the street door open

NEVER, NEVER, NEVER leave the black street door downstairs unclosed. Push it firmly shut from outside and check it twice. Watch the video to see how it must close.

Every single time

  • Not one hair on any bed. Check every bed carefully, sheets and pillows.
  • Leave the flat exactly as in the photos. Do not change the cushions or move anything.
  • Report any damage or anything missing the same day, with photos.
  • Anything you find that belongs to a guest stays theirs. Never throw it away or keep it. Report it to us.
  • Take all the rubbish out the day you finish the clean. Wednesday is bin collection day, for reference only.
  • Recycle as much as you can. The business is fined if recycling is left loose in the rubbish.
  • Close and lock the back windows when you leave: the king-size bedroom and the kitchen.
  • Change the outside code and update it in the system.

Do not touch

The small white device on the kitchen windowsillIt is the internet. If it is unplugged or moved, the Wi-Fi stops working.
The water valves in the bedroom with the king-size and single bedsThey are behind the small white panels on the wall. Leave them alone.
